# ergogen-footprints
This is my repo for all my ergogen footprints.
Most of them are straight from some online kicad footprint and then fed into Genture's [kicad2ergogen](https://kicad2ergogen.genteure.com/) with no editing whatsoever.
Some of them are verified to work with me fabricating a pcb with them and checking, but not all of them.

**Use at your own risk**
